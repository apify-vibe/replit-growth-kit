## Summary

1. The worst problem is structural. Competitor-teardown and demand-signal-scan both start paying (step 2 / step 3) before the plan is settled, and competitor-teardown still describes one spend form per angle group. The shared runtime's one-budget-per-job rule can't be followed as written (#1, #2).
2. Open-web-lead-engine's email verification logic contradicts itself in three places: a finder `Valid` scores as verified, the skip clause bypasses verification, and step 6 depends on step 7's sorting (#3, #4, #9). Gotchas also forbids the name-pattern guessing that the finder step relies on (#5).
3. In several places an example or a reference contradicts its own rule, and a cold reader copies the example: LinkedIn and TikTok brand-name inputs, YouTube tutorial searches, Instagram hashtag URLs, LinkedIn company size (#6, #10, #14, #15).
4. The shared `gotchas.md` and `replit-runtime.md` contradict each other on 401/403, partial-run retries and empty successful runs, and the $0.50 cap floor has no rule for when the remaining budget is below it (#8, #11, #12, #13).
5. The rest are undefined terms and stale text: an undocumented contact Actor, an undefined newsletter band rule, "Instagram adjacency", wrong step numbers and Reddit cap wording left from an older design. Each is a one-line fix.
